Vision, Mission & What We Offer

At ICECO, we aim to provide exceptional HVAC solutions that enhance the comfort, well-being, and efficiency of our clients ' spaces. We are committed to delivering the highest quality services, tailored to individual needs, while prioritizing sustainability and energy efficiency.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the epitome of professionalism in the HVAC industry. We aspire to set the highest standards for quality workmanship, ethics, and customer service. We see a future where professionalism is not just a trait but a guarantee in every interaction, making us the trusted choice for all HVAC needs

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver professional HVAC services with unwavering integrity, technical excellence, and unparalleled customer care. We are dedicated to providing efficient, reliable, and cost- effective solutions, ensuring the utmost comfort and satisfaction for our clients.

What We Offer

Heating Services, Cooling Services, Ventilation Services, Commercial and Residential Projects, Energy Efficiency Upgrades, Indoor Air Quality. We offer a maintenance plan, as well as emergency repairs.

Who We Are

In 1998, our journey began when Mr. Jean Saad established the company to specialize in the installation of basic air-conditioning systems. Over the years, our extensive experience allowed us to emerge as the industry leader in the Lebanese market. Today, we constantly deliver high-quality solutions and quick installations of all types of air conditioning systems such as VRV & Split, in some of Lebanon's most significant projects.

At the heart of our organization lies the core team. This team is a harmonious blend of expertise and dedication, comprising our esteemed founder alongside a cohort of highly skilled professionals; a dedicated group of mechanical engineers, foremen, skilled technicians, and laborers. Together, we stand as a unified force, working collaboratively to deliver the highest standards of quality and service to our valued clients.
